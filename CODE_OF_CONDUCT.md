# Code of conduct

Sci-Station contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, explain technical disagreements with evidence, credit others' work, and protect personal information and private research data. Participation is open regardless of background or experience.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing and repeated disruption are unacceptable in Issues, PRs and other project-managed spaces. Critique code and ideas without attacking people. Respect maintainers' moderation decisions and requests to stop unwanted contact.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict interaction, or ban participants depending on severity and repetition. They should explain the decision privately where feasible and protect reporters' identities.

For a conduct concern, contact maintainer [Funyday-k](https://github.com/Funyday-k) through a private contact method listed on the profile. If none is available, ask for a private channel in an Issue without naming affected people or describing sensitive details. Security vulnerabilities belong in the [private security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies to project-managed collaboration spaces and to people representing the project. Maintainers follow the same standards.
